# i have a monogram

![look at it :)](/assets/monogram.svg){alt="peter elliott's monogram"}


you may have seen it in the footer, or as this site's favicon.

## background

as a kid i was always interested in authenticating documents. seals, stamps
(postage and rubber), signatures, serial numbers, and eventually
cryptography. as i got back into crafts as an adult, i figured it would be cool
to have some kind of makers-mark to put on my stuff.

also, yes, my initials are P.E.E. my dad was unaware. my mom knew but didn't
think it would be a problem.

## designing the monogram

i first sketched out every arrangement of my initials i could think of:

![all of my monogram ideas (and some other peoples)](/projects/monogram/monogram-sketches.jpg)

pretty much finalized the design on paper, and then just tried to replicate it
in [inkscape](https://inkscape.org/).

## stamps

in order to transfer my design to the stamps, i printed them on to wax paper
double-sided-taped to a sheet of cardstock. I decided to try 3 sizes, ¼", ½" and 1".

![my printed templates. i smudged it after making the stamp.](/projects/monogram/monogram-templates.jpg)

if i was doing it again, i would probably just use wax paper alone. making this
sheet was a lot of work, and it didn't turn out to be reusable, as i'd hoped.

i then cut appropriate stamp blanks, and pressed them against the wet ink.

![after transferring the ink to the stamp blanks](/projects/monogram/uncarved-stamps.jpg)

at this point, i realized that i had no hope of carving the ¼" stamp.

i then outlined the monograms with my xacto knife. this was a mistake and i
should have started with a gouge. but i was still able to do a pretty
satisfactory job.

![completed ½" and 1" stamps](/projects/monogram/monogram-stamps.jpg)

![not bad considering the size of gouge i used](/projects/monogram/stamp-scale.jpg)

i'm pretty happy with these but i'll probably try to make better ones at some
point in the future.
