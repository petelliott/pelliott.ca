a friend of mine was talking to me about doing her nails for canada day. she
figured that she could probably paint a 3-pronged maple leaf, or worst case do a
little red blob (imagine the hypothetical flag of japanada). i figured i could
probably make a very small maple leaf stencil. now most of the time i figure i
can bang out a craft real quick, i am very wrong. but this time i was right!

so i went to wikipedia, downloaded an svg of the canadian flag, extracted the
maple leaf, scaled it to the right size, printed it and cut it out with my
trusty x-acto #11.

![a little wonky, but pretty good](/blog/canada-day-nails/paper-stencil.jpg){alt="a hand cut paper maple leaf stencil, about half the width of a thumbnail"}

wait how am i supposed to affix this to the nail? okay lets try that again. this
time i printed a few different sizes, to see which would look the best, and cut
through the paper onto some painters tape.

![](/blog/canada-day-nails/lined-up.jpg){width=33% alt="printed paper stencil taped overtop of painters tape"}![](/blog/canada-day-nails/cut.jpg){width=33% alt="stencil cut out of the paper"}![](/blog/canada-day-nails/stencil.jpg){width=33% alt="the weeded peeled up stencil"}

we stuck the painters tape on the nails, making sure every part was well adhered.

![oh yeah i had to make 2 (but i actually made like 5)](/blog/canada-day-nails/stencil-applied.jpg){alt="tape stencils applied to nails"}

she then painted the stencil

![so far so good](/blog/canada-day-nails/stencil-painted.jpg){alt="painted applied to stenciled nails"}

after the nail polish had partially dried, the tape was removed and voila!

![🇨🇦](/blog/canada-day-nails/done.jpg){alt="painted nails with stencils removed"}

I'm pretty happy with the result. they're a little blobby though, but the
internet says you can fix that by using a sponge to apply the nail polish, and
removing the tape immediately after to avoid the polish being pulled back off by
the tape.

![flag mode](/blog/canada-day-nails/flag.jpg){alt="canada flag nails, posed like the flag"}

anyway, I kind of want to try to design and cut some more complex, multi-color stencils now.
